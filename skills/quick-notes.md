# quick-notes

Remember anything the user says and find it again later.
Save: `remember` text with a short title (desktop: `memory.remember`). Find: `recall`
query (desktop: `memory.recall`) and answer from the note. Passwords, OTPs, PINs and card
numbers are refused by the tool — say that.
