# birthdays-and-dates

Remember birthdays and anniversaries and get reminded in time.
1. `remember` "<name> birthday: <date>".
2. `set_reminder` for the evening before at 20:00 and the day at 9:00.
3. On the day, offer to send a wish through whatsapp-message.
