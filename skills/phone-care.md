# phone-care

Battery, heat, slowness and storage: check and fix the usual causes.
1. `battery_status`, `phone_action` storage_status and memory_status.
2. Battery: offer battery_saver_panel and brightness_down; `phone_action` recent_apps so
   the user can close heavy apps.
3. Storage over 90%: say so, offer to open the gallery.
4. Hot phone: stop games or video, take the case off while charging.
Report what you found in two sentences.
