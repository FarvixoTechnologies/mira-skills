# nearby-places

Find nearby restaurants, ATMs, hospitals, petrol pumps and more.
1. `open_url` "https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=<what>+near+me".
2. For one place's opening hours or rating, Google Search it and answer in one line.
3. Hospital or danger: offer to call — see emergency-help.
