# homework-helper

Explain a school or college problem step by step instead of just giving answers.
1. Problem on the phone screen → `read_screen`; on the PC → `fs.read` the file they name.
2. Explain the idea, then the first step, and ask them to try the next.
3. Give the full answer only when asked or when they are stuck twice.
